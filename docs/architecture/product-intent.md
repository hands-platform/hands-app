# Product Intent

## Product Summary

`HANDS` is a realtime on-demand massage platform serving all of Vietnam.

The first MVP flow is intentionally simple:

- the customer sees nearby providers sorted by distance
- the customer opens one provider detail page
- the customer reviews profile, reviews, and services
- the customer chooses one service and sends one direct booking request
- the selected partner accepts or rejects that request
- chat opens after the provider accepts and starts the service flow

This is the primary Phase 1 direction.

## What Phase 1 Must Feel Like

The customer journey should feel like:

1. nearby provider list
2. provider detail
3. service selection
4. booking request
5. provider response
6. chat and service tracking

The provider journey should feel like:

1. login
2. online presence
3. direct request inbox
4. accept or reject
5. start service
6. chat and delivery workflow

## Why We Are Starting Here

We are keeping the first live workflow easy to understand because it is:

- faster to validate
- easier to explain to partners and staff
- easier to test with real users
- lower risk than full multi-provider open matching

The broader multi-provider open matching concept remains valuable, but it is a later-stage capability, not the primary first customer flow.

## UX Direction Before Figma

Until the final design file arrives, the product should stay:

- easy to scan
- easy to demo
- easy to reason about
- operationally obvious

That means:

- clear primary actions
- obvious request states
- simple provider cards
- direct booking language
- minimal decorative styling
