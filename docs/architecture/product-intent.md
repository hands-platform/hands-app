# Product Intent

## Product Summary

`HANDS` is a realtime on-demand massage platform serving all of Vietnam.

The first MVP flow is intentionally simple:

- the customer sees nearby partners sorted by distance
- the customer opens one partner detail page
- the customer reviews profile, factual feedback records, and services
- the customer chooses one service and sends one first-pick booking request
- the first-pick partner has a 10 minute response window
- marketplace partners within the configured radius can join during that same window
- the customer always selects the final partner
- matched chat opens after the customer selects the final partner

This is the primary Phase 1 direction.

## What Phase 1 Must Feel Like

The customer journey should feel like:

1. nearby partner list
2. partner detail
3. service selection
4. booking request
5. first-pick response plus marketplace partner candidates
6. customer final partner selection
7. chat and service tracking

The partner journey should feel like:

1. login
2. online presence
3. direct first-pick request inbox
4. open marketplace request list
5. accept, reject, or join
6. start service
7. chat and delivery workflow

## Why We Are Starting Here

We are keeping the first live workflow easy to understand because it is:

- faster to validate
- easier to explain to partners and staff
- easier to test with real users
- easier to operate than automatic marketplace matching

The customer still keeps control: partners can join as marketplace candidates, but HANDS does not automatically assign the final partner.

## UX Direction Before Figma

Until the final design file arrives, the product should stay:

- easy to scan
- easy to demo
- easy to reason about
- operationally obvious

That means:

- clear primary actions
- obvious request states
- simple partner cards
- first-pick and marketplace matching language
- minimal decorative styling
